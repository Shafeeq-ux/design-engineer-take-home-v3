07 · How the flow is built

From the question to a change on Home.

  1. 1ComposerJordan asks from Home
  2. 2streamAnswerStatus, three sentences, the card, done
  3. 3AnswerScores 4.1, 3.8, and 2.9
  4. 4EvidenceOne score open, one quote

Then he acts on the gap

  1. 5DraftHe can edit the note and remove what Marcus sees
  2. 6SendNothing leaves until this button
  3. 7ReceiptAs drafted, or with his edits
  4. 8HomeBooks 9:00 AM. Follow-ups move from 4 to 5

Opening a score moves the stage from Answer to Evidence. One row stays open.

Dock is optional and lasts for this visit. Reopening starts over the brief.

Escape, Not now, and Recall close the sheet and return him to the question.

09 · Interaction decisions

Three interactions, each doing one job.

10 · Accessibility

He can finish this without a mouse.

  • Escape closes it from any stage and returns him to the question. While it floats, Tab stays inside. Docked, Home stays in the tab order.
  • Focus is visible. When the sheet opens it lands on Close, and the ring appears on the next Tab.
  • A screen reader hears one summary when the card is ready, naming all three scores. Streamed sentences are not read out one by one.
  • Each score is a button. It says the value out of 5, and whether the evidence is open.
  • Reduced motion keeps the stages. The sheet, the dock, and the close do not travel.

12 · Design decisions

What I tried, what I dropped, and what stayed.

Tried

  • A chain of thought
  • A progress bar
  • A retrieval inspector
  • A rich text editor
  • A radar chart
  • A calendar
  • Several rehearsal times

Dropped

  • The rich text editor

    The coaching note should stay simple and crisp.

  • The calendar

    Tomorrow at 9:00 AM was already free for both of them.

  • A radar chart

    A radar is for multivariate data: three or more quantitative variables. That dataset was not in the mock.

  • Several rehearsal times

    One open slot was enough. Booking is one confirmation.

Kept

  • The progress bar

    He can see all five stages, and which one he is in.

  • The evidence under the score

    He is about to coach someone, so the sentence behind the number is the part of an inspector worth keeping.

13 · Engineering decisions

Built on the stack that was already here.

  • The new pieces use the shell’s type, color, buttons, and the question bar.
  • React and TypeScript, which the project already used.
  • No assistant UI kit. The highlighted files are the ones added for this flow.
  • Working rules live in DIRECTIONS.md, so the next person does not put back what was taken out.

14 · With more time

I would fix the undo first.

1

Recall should bring the note back.

Today it says nothing was sent, then closes the assistant. The control promises an undo. It should put the note back in Draft.

2

Let him open the call itself.

He can read the reason. The practice data has no recording to open, so I would not invent a link that goes nowhere.

3

Show how he compares with the team.

A benchmark beside each score is the next step in DECISIONS.md. That needs numbers we were not given.

The receipt still prints 9:12 and 9:14. Those should be the time he actually sent, not fixed copy.
